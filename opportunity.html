Join Our Team

We are continuously seeking talented individuals from around the world to join our research group. We welcome applicants who are passionate about Artificial Intelligence, Large Language Models, Multimodal Learning, Document Intelligence, Data Science, and AI for Science.

Our former team members have continued their careers at top universities and companies worldwide, including but not limited to CMU, UIUC, NUS, University of Washington (UW), Tsinghua University, Peking University, as well as Meta, Google, Microsoft, Alibaba, ByteDance, SenseTime, and many others.

Current openings include Joint PhD Students, Research Scientists, and Research Interns.

Joint Ph.D. Students

We collaborate with leading universities to jointly train PhD students, including Tsinghua University, Peking University, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Fudan University, Zhejiang University, University of Science and Technology of China, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Tongji University, among others.

Students will conduct cutting-edge research under joint supervision and participate in major research projects.

For more information, please visit:
https://www.shlab.org.cn/enrollment

Research Interns

We welcome research interns to join our daily research activities, including model development, algorithm design, dataset construction, and academic writing.

Outstanding interns will have opportunities to publish high-quality papers as lead or co-lead authors.
